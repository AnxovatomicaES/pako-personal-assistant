# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. Assume good faith, disagree about ideas and not about people, and keep discussions about the project.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not accepted. Maintainers may remove comments, close
issues and block people who do that. To report a problem, use a private security advisory or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This is a short form of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which applies in full.
