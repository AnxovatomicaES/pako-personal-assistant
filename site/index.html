What it does

A real assistant, not a chatbot.

🧠

It remembers people

Who Ana is, when her birthday is, that the meeting with Rohan moved to Thursday. Every fact is dated, so Pako can tell you what changed.

📌

Follows up for you

What you were asked or promised on Slack, in mail or in a meeting. It checks, and only speaks if it is not done.

☀️

A morning brief

At 08:00: your day, the to-dos with their story, what moved since yesterday. Every line cites its source.

🍽️

Books restaurants

Real places from Google Maps, ranked. You pick one, press once, and Pako writes to them.

✉️

Reads and sends mail

Only what matters reaches you. Scams flagged, codes hidden. It emails your contacts when you ask.

📞

Proactive, not noisy

It speaks first only when it adds something.

Every half hour Pako looks at your day and may say one thing: a commitment you may be missing, a birthday tomorrow, a slot for your climb. Nothing is the usual answer. Never in your quiet hours, never in a meeting, never twice on one topic.

Your sister's birthday is tomorrow. Want me to draft a message you send yourself?

You keep the last word

Powerful, and on a short leash.

Press it. One word halts every call and every outgoing message, and it survives a restart.

  • Other people's words are information, never orders. An email or a web page cannot make Pako act: anything written after reading them waits for your press.
  • Everything it changes comes with Undo. A message you asked for leaves after a few seconds with an Undo button, or always with a card first if you prefer.
  • Your data stays home. Everything Pako keeps is in one folder on your computer. Export it, or delete everything, in one click.
  • It says it is an AI. Every call opens saying so, and every email it sends for you says so.

Download for Mac

macOS 13 or newer, Intel or Apple silicon. This preview is not yet signed by Apple: the first time, macOS says it could not verify Pako. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway next to Pako, and confirm. Once.

One line. It checks Node.js and git, installs Pako in ~/pako, starts it and opens the panel. Never sudo.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnxovatomicaES/pako-personal-assistant/main/install.sh | sh

Needs Node.js 22.18 or newer and Claude Code signed in to your plan.

In PowerShell 5.1 or 7:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnxovatomicaES/pako-personal-assistant/main/install.ps1 | iex

Needs Node.js 22.18 or newer, git, and Claude Code signed in to your plan. The installer is run on a real Windows machine on every change.

Questions

Plainly answered.

What do I need for Claude?

Claude Code, Anthropic's official command-line tool, installed and signed in by you: with your own Claude plan or with your own Anthropic API key. Usage counts against your plan's limits, or is billed to your key, under Anthropic's terms. If you use Pako for work, use a Claude Team or Enterprise plan or an API key: in the EU, Anthropic's consumer plans are for personal use.

What does it cost?

Pako is free (MIT license). Claude is paid by you to Anthropic. Everything else is optional.

Does my computer have to stay on?

Yes: Pako runs where you start it, and it must be on and awake to answer. What came in while it slept is caught up when it wakes, and late reminders say so.

Where is my data?

In one folder on your computer, readable only by you. What you write goes to Anthropic through Claude, and to a service only if you connect it. Export or delete everything from the panel.

Can someone else's email make Pako do things?

No. Other people's words are read as information. Anything Pako would do after reading them waits for your press on a card.

Is it for teams?

No. One Pako serves one person. That is the point.
