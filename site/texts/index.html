12 follow-up texts you can copy

Twelve messages for the moments everyone avoids. Copy them word for word, change the names and the times.

Chasing something you were promised

1 The first nudge

Hi Sam, picking this up again: are you still good to send the numbers by Thursday? If Thursday's tight, tell me the real day and I'll plan around it.

2 The second nudge (no guilt)

Hi Sam, I'm putting the report together tomorrow at 10. If the numbers are in by then they go in; if not I'll mark that section as pending and we add it next week. Which do you prefer?

3 When "I'll look into it" went quiet

Quick one on the invoice question from last week: did you get a chance to look? A yes, a no or a 'not before the 5th' are all fine. I just need to know which.

4 After a meeting with no owner

Thanks all. So nothing gets lost: Maya sends the deck by Thursday, Leo books the room for the 12th, I confirm the budget by Friday. Shout if I got one wrong.

Owning something you promised

5 It's already late

I said Friday and it's Monday. That's on me. You'll have it by 3 pm today.

Want the next ones? One short email on Sunday evening.

Email me in

6 It's going to be late

Heads up before it's a surprise: the draft won't be ready tomorrow. Thursday at noon is realistic. Does that still work, or should I send what I have?

7 You forgot completely

I dropped this one, sorry. I've just read it properly. Here's my answer: …

8 You can't do it after all

I said I'd take this and I can't do it well this month. I'd rather tell you now than deliver late. Can I hand you my notes so far?

Closing the loop

9 Done, in one line

Done: the contract is signed and in the shared folder. Nothing else needed from you.

10 Saying no without a maybe

I'm going to say no to this one so I don't half-do it. If it's still open in March, ask me again.

11 Turning "let's catch up" into a date

Let's make it real: Tuesday 12:30 or Thursday 17:00? If neither works, send me two times that do.

12 Before you log off

I'm away until the 2nd. Open on my side: the Q4 summary (Maya has it while I'm out) and the vendor call (moved to the 4th). Everything else can wait.

The pattern in all twelve: a name, a verb, a time. Say what happens next, and by when.
