Privacy

Version of 17 November 2026. Changes are versioned in Pako's repository.

The Pako project receives none of your data. Pako is software you run on your own computer. There is no Pako account, no Pako server, no telemetry, no analytics and no project-owned keys. You, the person who installed it, decide what it connects to.

If you use Pako for work, your employer's rules apply to the work data it reads (mail, Slack, Zoom, calendars). Ask your IT or privacy team first, and use a Claude Team or Enterprise plan or an API key: in the EU, Anthropic's consumer plans are for personal use.

1. What Pako keeps on your computer

Everything Pako keeps is in one folder, ~/.pako, readable only by your user account: your notes and reminders, lists and tasks, the people in your life you told it about, the chat, what it read for you (short summaries, never whole emails), its activity log, and the secrets of the connections you set up. Each kind of item has a set lifetime and is deleted by itself after it (from 30 minutes for one-time codes to about a year for your notes). The full table is in docs/privacy.md, and the panel's Privacy and your data card shows it on your own computer. We recommend turning on FileVault on a Mac.

3. The health diary (Labs, off by default)

Pako is not a medical device. Its health diary is a personal notebook: it stores and summarizes what you write so you can remember it and show it to your doctor. It does not diagnose, assess, monitor or treat any condition, and it does not replace a doctor. If you think you may have an emergency, call your local emergency number. The diary stays on your computer and is never written to a log; when Claude reads it to answer you, that part goes to Anthropic. FORGET MY HEALTH deletes it.

4. Other people

Pako notes what you tell it about people in your life, and what it reads in your own mail, calendar, Slack and Zoom (names and the addresses they wrote from, never what they wrote, beyond short summaries for you). Every call and every email Pako sends for you says it comes from your AI assistant. Anyone can ask you to delete what Pako noted about them: FORGET PERSON does it.

5. Keeping and deleting

In the panel's Privacy card: Export my data (one file with everything, no secrets) and Delete everything. What each provider keeps on its side (for example Claude Code's own history in ~/.claude, or provider logs) follows that provider's rules.

6. Your rights

Your local copy is yours to read, export and delete at any time. Towards each provider you have the rights their policies and the GDPR give you (access, deletion, objection). You can complain to your local data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

7. Security and contact

How Pako protects your data and how to report a problem: SECURITY.md. Questions about this notice: open an issue in Pako's repository, without personal data.
